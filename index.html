About

About me

I am a Remote Sensing and GIS researcher working at the intersection of hydrology, land surface modelling and climate science. I integrate Earth Observation, machine learning and open-source tools to address environmental challenges and inform evidence-based policy. I hold a Ph.D. in Environmental and Water Resources Engineering from the National Institute of Technology, Jamshedpur, and I am currently a Post-Doctoral Research Associate at the Centre for Narmada River Basin Management Studies, IIT Indore, a Ministry of Jal Shakti project under the National River Conservation Plan.

My research contributions span ten journal articles, three book chapters, a national monograph, an authored book, eight technical reports for the Ministry of Jal Shakti, Government of India, and five filed patent applications. I lead a multidisciplinary team of fourteen members, coordinate research activities, and mentor undergraduate and postgraduate students.

My long-term goal is to contribute to the United Nations’ work in shaping science-informed environmental policies that address global challenges at the intersection of water, land and climate. I aim to bridge Earth Observation and AI-driven research with environmental law, governance and international policy to advance humanitarian action, global sustainability and equitable decision-making.

Inspired by the teachings of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, I believe that education is the key to unlocking one's potential. His vision resonates with my commitment to women's empowerment and education. I strive to promote equal opportunities for all, because empowering individuals through education is essential for both personal and societal progress.

Through this website I share my research and experiences with fellow researchers and enthusiasts. Thank you for visiting. I look forward to connecting with you!

Research

Research domains

My work applies Earth Observation, geospatial technology and machine learning to water, land and climate, from river basins and erosion to riparian zones, water quality, hydroclimatic extremes and decision support.

Soil erosion modelling

  • Theoretical, statistical and physically based soil erosion modelling
  • Landslide susceptibility modelling
  • Quantification of river basin erosion and accretion (see the QGIS plugin)

Urbanisation impact analysis

  • Remote sensing and geospatial technology based urbanisation and land use and land cover change modelling
  • Implementation of SVM, Random Forest and CA-ANN for urbanisation analysis and modelling

Hydrological modelling of river basins

  • Geospatial technologies for large-scale streamflow analysis
  • Physically based hydrological modelling using SWAT
  • Satellite-based modelling of soil moisture and agricultural water stress in the Narmada basin

Geomorphological river basin analysis

  • Geospatial technologies for geomorphological analysis of river basins
  • Hypsometric and morphometric characterisation of large-scale river basins

Hydro-geologically constrained groundwater zones

  • Classification of areas by depth to water level (DWL)
  • A methodology to delineate zones of hydro-geological constraint

Forestry and coastal biodiversity modelling

  • Environmental impact assessment
  • Coastal biodiversity modelling
  • Mangrove deforestation, carbon dynamics and the vulnerability of coastal communities in the Sundarbans
  • Heatwave-induced forest fires in tropical ecoregions

Air pollution monitoring and assessment

  • Aerosol and particulate matter pollution modelling
  • Identifying dominant sources of particulate matter pollution

Riparian zones and shoreline change

  • Earth Observation based delineation, functional characterisation and condition assessment of riparian zones across climatic zones
  • Climatic vulnerability of seasonal riparian zones
  • Long-term shoreline change mapping with Google Earth Engine (ShorelineDetect)

Water quality and pollution

  • Earth Observation and machine learning based water quality modelling
  • Responses of water quality to basin morphometry and climatic extremes
  • Automated analysis of urban and territorial surface water bodies
  • Graph neural network based identification and prioritisation of polluted river stretches

Hydroclimatic extremes and risk

  • Climate variability and extremes in Indian river basins: trends, regional disparities and urban risk
  • Extreme-value estimation in streamflow records
  • Drought analysis and forecasting; snow cover patterns in the Sutlej basin
  • Land-use transitions, hydrological futures and runoff projections in the Krishna basin

Planning and decision support

  • Technical reports on the Narmada river basin for the Ministry of Jal Shakti (topography, lithology, geomorphology, hydrology, river atlas)
  • AI-enabled emergency water supply planning for drought and contamination events
  • Urban livability and investability assessment from multi-source Earth Observation data

Geostatistics

  • Geostatistical analysis for river basin modelling
  • Spatial geostatistics analysis
